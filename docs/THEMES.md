# DrakOps appearance: imported palette origins

DrakOps includes Learning Lab Navy, five stock DLMS palettes, and 22
palettes from [Omarchy v4.0.4](https://github.com/omacom/omarchy/releases/tag/v4.0.4).
DLMS Ethereal and Omarchy Ethereal are separate palettes.

- DLMS: Dark, Light, Purple & Gold, Maroon & Gold, and Ethereal. The
  imported palette material retains its supplied MIT notice in
  `Notices/LICENSE.dlms`.
- Omarchy: the source is pinned to [commit c668141](https://github.com/omacom/omarchy/tree/c668141e9c42b13c80c9ca4ea108e11708c5e8a5).
  The imported palette material retains its upstream MIT notice in
  `Notices/LICENSE.omarchy`.
- [DLMS Learning Lab source](https://github.com/drakahari/dlms_learning_lab/tree/6b54e34869e031e50d6cf0854cce3402f65c6fd4)
  supplied the theme renderer and inventory context.

The retained MIT notices apply to the respective imported materials. This
document does not grant a license to DrakOps' original code. The palettes
change DrakOps' appearance; they do not install system themes.
